Welcome to the One Star Wonder!

We are the One Star Wonder, where it's a wonder how we still have that star!

We are located in the heart of the middle of nowhere in North Carolina.

The surroundings are forests with absolutely nobody, or anything around, not even a restaurant, for miles!

Don't think you will find anything to eat here, as we do not serve breakfast, lunch, or dinner.

Our rooms are large enough to only fit a moldy mattress.
