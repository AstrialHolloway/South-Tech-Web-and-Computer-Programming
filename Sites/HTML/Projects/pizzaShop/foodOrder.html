Menu

Fresh favorites for the whole family! Build your own pizza or pick up a snack, a sweet treat, and a drink.

Build Your Own Pizza

Choose a crust, sauce, cheese, and all your favorite toppings.

  • Crust: Hand-tossed, thin, or pan
  • Sauce: Classic tomato, barbecue, or garlic white sauce
  • Cheese: Mozzarella, cheddar, or extra cheese
  • Meat toppings: Pepperoni, sausage, ham, bacon, grilled chicken, or meatballs
  • Veggie toppings: Mushrooms, green peppers, red onions, black olives, green olives, tomatoes, spinach, or jalapeños
  • Finishing touches: Pineapple, garlic, or crushed red pepper

Specialty Pizzas

  • Showbiz Supreme: Pepperoni, sausage, mushrooms, green peppers, and onions
  • Meat Lover's: Pepperoni, sausage, ham, bacon, and meatballs
  • Garden Party: Mushrooms, green peppers, onions, black olives, and tomatoes
