How To Play

Guess the hidden word before running out of tries! Any valid English dictionary word matching the puzzle's length is accepted—and for 9+ letter words or Custom Wordles, any matching-length combination of letters is allowed.

  • Daily 5: Solve 5 progressive daily words every day — starting at 3 letters (#1) up to 7 letters (#5).
  • Endless: Play an infinite stream of random words (including 9-letter wildcard rounds).
  • Custom: Build your own puzzle up to 50 letters and 15 guesses, or paste a friend's code to play theirs.

Tile Examples

W Green: Letter is in the word and in the exact right spot.
O Yellow: Letter is in the word, but in a different spot.
R Gray: Letter is not in the word in any spot.
