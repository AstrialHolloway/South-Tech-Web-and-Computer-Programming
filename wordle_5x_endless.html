Splendid! Word: APPLE

How To Play

Guess the hidden word in 6 tries. Each guess must be a valid word matching the puzzle's length.

  • Daily 5 Puzzles: Solve 5 progressive daily words every day — starting at 3 letters (Puzzle 1) up to 7 letters (Puzzle 5).
  • Endless Stream: Play an infinite stream of randomly generated words at any length!

Tile Examples

W Green: Letter is in the word and in the exact right spot.
O Yellow: Letter is in the word, but in a different spot.
R Gray: Letter is not in the word in any spot.
